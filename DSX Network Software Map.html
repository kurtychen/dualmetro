What the map shows

  • Four design vendors anchor the builder side

    Dassault Systèmes, Cadence, Siemens and PTC all partner with NVIDIA on DSX, and they hold most of the named ties to equipment makers, system builders and Switch. Dassault Systèmes leads on configurators (Vertiv, QCT, Pegatron) and Cadence on thermal twins (HPE, Foxconn, Switch).

  • Emerald AI is the hub on the grid side

    It has more named ties than any other vendor: Digital Realty, Nebius and Nscale; the grid bodies EPRI, National Grid, PJM and Silicon Valley Power; five power producers; and investments from Eaton and GE Vernova. DSX Flex runs through it.

  • DSX partners are buying software companies

    Schneider Electric owns AVEVA and ETAP and has agreed to buy PTC. IREN completed its purchase of Mirantis, a DSX OS integrator, on Aug 4, 2026. Trane owns BrainBox AI.

  • Most AI clouds have no outside software tie on record

    Only 5 of 24 AI clouds have one: CoreWeave, IREN, Nscale, Nebius and Aolani, through VAST Data, Mirantis, BE Networks, Phaidra, Emerald AI and Rafay. The rest appear only as adopters of NVIDIA’s own DSX OS, MaxLPS or DSX Air.

Method and limits

The DSX network is every company NVIDIA has named in DSX announcements from the Omniverse DSX Blueprint launch (Oct 28, 2025) to GTC Berlin (Oct 21, 2026), plus companies that announced DSX adoption themselves, such as Switch, ABB, Flex, Aolani and BE Networks. NVIDIA’s DSX Sim, Air, OS, Flex and Ready pages were checked for partner lists.

A tie counts only when a public source names both companies together. A named partnership is a joint announcement. Documented tool use is a case study or a published stack that names the vendor. Ownership covers subsidiaries, completed or pending acquisitions, and strategic investments. Ecosystem links come from NVIDIA statements that list several companies at once.

Not covered: the 2,000+ SimReady assets on Hugging Face, whose contributors are mostly unnamed; NVIDIA Partner Network members with no DSX mention; and vendor-to-vendor ties such as Siemens’ investment in Emerald AI. No DSX-specific tie was found for Autodesk, Bentley Systems, Hexagon or Trimble.

“None found” means no public record as of Oct 23, 2026. Many cloud partners appear only once, in NVIDIA’s lists of DSX OS or MaxLPS adopters. In-house platforms (Foxconn FODT, Pegatron PEGAVERSE, Wistron AIF-DT, Delta DIATwin, Eaton Brightlayer, Hitachi Energy’s energy management system, Siemens Energy Noedra, Switch LDC EVO) are noted on each company but not drawn as vendors.
